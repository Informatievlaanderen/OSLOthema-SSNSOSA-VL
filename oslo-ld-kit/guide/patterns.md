# Recurring patterns

Patterns that more than one domain has used. Before modelling a new domain, check whether one of
these fits. Then choose consciously: follow the pattern (consistency across domains), or model the
domain's own reading and explain why. When both are defensible, make an alternative mapping (skill
`alternative-mapping`) instead of choosing silently.

The *used in* column names data examples in OSLOthema-SSNSOSA-VL (`src/main/input/`).

| Pattern | Core | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| [Emission as feature of interest](#emission-as-feature-of-interest) | the emission is the object of study, generated by an activity | waterkwaliteit (discharge), geluidsmeetnet `alternatief/`, overstort `alternatief/` |
| [Stimulus](#stimulus) | the event that makes a sensor measure | geluidsmeetnet (main model) |
| [Sample with ultimate feature of interest](#sample-with-ultimate-feature-of-interest) | the sample is the feature of interest, the sampled object the ultimate one | waterkwaliteit, paleo |
| [Spatial sample](#spatial-sample) | a measuring point as sample of a larger area | verkeersmetingen, waterkwaliteit (sampling point), geluidsmeetnet |
| [Derived observation](#derived-observation) | computed from other observations' results | verkeersmetingen (daily total), geluidsmeetnet (Lden), paleo, waterkwaliteit |
| [Reporting limit](#reporting-limit) | "< X" as an interval, not as a value | waterkwaliteit |

## Emission as feature of interest

```turtle
ex:discharge-2400006 a prov:Entity , sosa:FeatureOfInterest ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy ex:company-activity ;      # or a flight, a production run, …
    geo:hasGeometry ex:geom-sampling-well-2400006 . # or prov:atLocation, see below
ex:observation-… sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest ex:discharge-2400006 .
```

The emission (a discharge, a noise event) is a `prov:Entity` and the feature of interest; the
activity that caused it is a `prov:Activity` (`prov:wasGeneratedBy`); where it was measured is not
the feature of interest. Give the emission a position with `geo:hasGeometry` when coordinates are
known (it may share the measuring point's `geo:Geometry`); `prov:atLocation` to the measuring point
is not wrong, and is the choice when the place is only known by an address, a description or a
resource without coordinates (R11, *Where a feature of interest is*). The emission is timeless: the period
lies on the observation's `sosa:phenomenonTime`, and the matrix (waste water, surface water) is a
property of the feature of interest.

*Choose it* when the question is about the source and its output (loads, emissions per company or
per flight). *Rather not* when the question is about the state of a place.

**Passive source.** When nothing is steered (a combined sewer overflow spilling over a weir), the
activity that generates the emission can be a `sosa:Actuation` on the structure, with the emission
as its result, a `sosa:ActuatingProcedure`, and no `sosa:madeByActuator`. One resource then serves
both as `prov:wasGeneratedBy` and as `sosa:isResultOf` (`sosa:Actuation ⊂ prov:Activity`,
`sosa:hasResult ⊂ prov:generated`).

**Events without an emission.** A data stream may also report periods *without* an emission (no
overflow, signal disturbed). Those have no emission to be the feature of interest; they stay
observations of the place. The feature of interest then depends on the status of the event, which
is an argument against this pattern for such streams (overstort `alternatief/`, discussion §6).

## Stimulus

```turtle
ex:noise-event-… a sosa:Stimulus , prov:Entity ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy ex:flight-… ;
    sosa:isDetectedBy ex:sound-level-meter-M072 .
ex:event-collection-… sosa:wasOriginatedBy ex:noise-event-… ;
    sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest ex:monitoring-station-M072 .
```

SOSA 2023 defines a stimulus as the real-world event that makes a sensor observe. The feature of
interest stays the place where the effect is measured, which keeps the *source* apart from the
*place of measurement*. It follows SOSA's own sensing chain (stimulus → sensor → observation).

*Choose it* when the event triggers a measurement at a fixed station and the station's state is the
question. Compare with the emission pattern: in OSLOthema-SSNSOSA-VL both readings of the noise
data exist side by side (`geluidsmeetnet/` and `geluidsmeetnet/alternatief/`).

## Sample with ultimate feature of interest

The analysed sample is the feature of interest of the analysis; the sampled object (a discharge, a
river, an ice core) is reachable with `sosa:isSampleOf` and stated as
`sosa:hasUltimateFeatureOfInterest` for short query paths (R8). The sampling is a `sosa:Sampling`
with the sample as its result.

## Spatial sample

A measuring point that stands for a larger area is a `sosa:Platform`, `sosa:FeatureOfInterest` and
`sosa:SpatialSample`, `sosa:isSampleOf` the area, with a geometry (R11). The collection's feature of
interest is the point, not the area.

## Derived observation

`sosa:hasInputValue` to the source *results*, `sosa:relatedObservation` to the source
*observations*; not a member of the source collection; all required properties stated explicitly
(R7, R13). Chains are allowed: Lden is derived from Ld, Le and Ln, themselves derived.

## Reporting limit

```turtle
ex:result-… qudt:lowerBound 0.0 ; qudt:upperBound 0.0125 ; qudt:hasUnit unit:MicroGM-PER-L .
```

A value below the reporting limit has no `qudt:numericValue`: a value equal to the limit would
suggest a measurement that does not exist. Give the interval the laboratory reports (`[0, X]` for
"< X", `[v, v]` for an exact value). The sign follows from the presence or absence of
`numericValue`; no separate sign code list is needed. The detection and quantification limits
themselves are a separate, still open question (candidate: `ssn-system:DetectionLimit`).
